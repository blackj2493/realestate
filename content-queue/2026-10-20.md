**Title:** A third of active Whitby listings have already cut their asking price (33%, n=458)

**Body:**

Pulled the Whitby numbers this morning: **33% of active Whitby listings have cut their
asking price**, with a typical reduction of 7.4%. Sample is 458 active listings.

That's the highest price-cut share of the 15 Ontario markets I track today. It's the kind
of thing a headline "average price" smooths over — a home relisted lower still prints as
one active listing at its new number, so the share of sellers who've already blinked gets
averaged away.

For prior art: TRREB publishes average and benchmark prices monthly, and HouseSigma shows
the price history on each individual property. What I measure is different — the share of
active Whitby inventory that has taken at least one cut, computed per region and refreshed
nightly. Not better than those; just the cut the board-level averages hide.

I'm a licensed Ontario realtor and I built PureProperty — a free data tool — because the
board sits on numbers like this and the consumer sites won't show them.

Tracker (updated nightly): https://www.pureproperty.ca/data/price-cuts

AMA, or tell me where I'm reading this wrong — is a 33% cut-share in Whitby distress, or
just sellers who started high heading into fall?
