**Title:** The typical active Whitby listing has now sat 16 days on the true clock — longest of any market I track today

**Body:**

Everyone quotes "days on market," but the board resets that clock every time a listing is cancelled and relisted — so the number you see on the consumer sites is often a fiction that reads younger than the listing actually is. When I stitch the history back together and measure *true* days on market, **the typical active Whitby listing has now sat 16 days.** That's the longest median true DOM of the markets I track today (n=19 active).

Sixteen isn't dramatic on its own — but it's the *longest* today, and more to the point it's the *honest* number. A listing showing "3 days" on a portal after a quiet relist is a seller two-plus weeks into carrying costs, not a fresh arrival. That gap is exactly where an offer finds room.

Disclosure: I'm a licensed Ontario realtor and I built PureProperty — a free data tool — because the board sits on numbers like this and the consumer sites won't show them. The relisting reset is the single most useful thing the MLS system obscures, so I rebuilt the honest clock and put it up for free: https://www.pureproperty.ca/data/days-on-market

If you're active in Whitby: does 16 feel right once you account for relists, or are detached vs condo pulling the median in different directions? AMA / tell me where I'm wrong.
