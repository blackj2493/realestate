- **Region:** Oakville · **Sample:** n = 1024 active listings
- **Source:** https://www.pureproperty.ca/data/days-on-market
- **Why surprising:** Longest median true days-on-market of the markets tracked today.

### (a) Reddit — r/TorontoRealEstate

**Title:** Median *true* days-on-market for active Oakville listings is now 77 days — longest of the markets I track

**Body:**

Median **true** days-on-market for currently-active Oakville listings is **77 days** — the longest of the markets in the tracker today (n = 1024 active).

The word doing the work is *true*. The DOM you see on a portal resets to 0 the moment a listing is terminated and re-entered, so a home that's actually been shopping for four months can show up looking a week old. The true figure stitches the relists back together, which is why 77 is higher than the on-listing counter would tell you.

Prior art, to be fair: days-on-market itself is on every portal, and HouseSigma surfaces relist history per listing if you open it. What the tracker does differently is carry the stitched median for the whole market and refresh it nightly.

Rotation slot for today is the method type, and this angle is the cleanest method story of the four: why the days-on-market number most people quote is fiction.

```
Days-on-market is one of the most-quoted housing numbers, and one of the easiest to break.

A listing that's terminated and re-entered starts its counter over at zero. So a home that's genuinely been shopping for months can display as if it hit the market last week — and any median built on the displayed counter inherits that reset.

Stitch the relists back into a single continuous run and the picture changes. Median true days-on-market for active Oakville listings is now 77 — the longest of the Ontario markets we track. The on-portal counter would show you something shorter and calmer.

This is a share-vs-substance problem the whole industry lives with: the metric everyone cites is the one the board's relist mechanics quietly distort. We publish the stitched version, nightly, so the median means what it says.

Neighbourhood table, updated nightly: pureproperty.ca/data/days-on-market
```
