## Angle 3 — Richmond Hill True days-on-market

- **Figure (verbatim):** `72 days`
- **Region:** Richmond Hill · **Sample:** n=1031 active listings
- **Source:** https://www.pureproperty.ca/data/days-on-market
- **Why it's surprising:** Longest median true days-on-market of the markets tracked today.

### (a) Reddit — r/TorontoRealEstate

**Title:** The typical active Richmond Hill listing has now sat 72 days (measured so relists don't reset the clock)

**Body:**

> Median true days-on-market for active Richmond Hill listings is 72 days across 1,031
> listings — the longest of the markets I'm tracking today.
>
> The word doing the work is *true*. The standard DOM you see on a listing resets to
> zero every time a property is terminated and re-listed, which is exactly what happens
> to a stale listing that isn't moving. TRREB and CREA publish average DOM in the
> monthly stats, and HouseSigma shows it per listing — what I'm doing differently is
> stitching the relist history back together so the clock keeps running, then taking the
> median at the municipal level nightly.
>
> 72 days is a median, so half of active Richmond Hill inventory has sat longer than
> that. It's a measure of how long stock is taking to move, not a prediction about price.
>
> I'm a licensed Ontario realtor and I built PureProperty — a free data tool — because
> the board sits on numbers like this and the consumer sites won't show them.
>
> AMA — and push back if you think relist-stitching over-counts: where's the line
> between a genuine relist and a legitimately new listing of the same address?

**Self-score: 86/100.** The method (relist-stitching) is the differentiator and it's
explained without jargon; prior art named twice (TRREB/CREA, HouseSigma) with the
