- **Figure (verbatim):** `67 days`
- **Region:** Hamilton · **Sample:** n=2,268
- **Source URL:** https://www.pureproperty.ca/data/days-on-market
- **Why surprising:** Longest median true days-on-market of the markets tracked today —
  and MLS days-on-market usually reads far lower because it resets on relist.

### (a) Reddit — r/TorontoRealEstate

> **The typical active Hamilton listing has now sat 67 days**
>
> That's the longest median true days-on-market of the markets I track today, across
> 2,268 active listings.
>
> Why this number is usually invisible: MLS days-on-market resets to zero every time a
> listing is cancelled and re-posted, so the public figure can read "5 days" on a home
> that's actually been for sale since the summer. True DOM stitches those relists back
> together so the clock doesn't restart.
>
> For prior art: TRREB publishes average days-on-market in its monthly Market Watch. The
> difference here is a per-region *median* True DOM that survives relisting, refreshed
> nightly rather than monthly.

### (d) LinkedIn — Type C (The method)

> MLS days-on-market is one of the most quoted numbers in real estate, and one of the
> easiest to break.
>
> Every time a listing is cancelled and re-posted, the counter resets to zero. A home
> that's been for sale since August can show "5 days" the morning after a relist.
>
> So the public figure systematically understates how long inventory actually sits — and
> it does so most in exactly the slow markets where that gap matters.
>
> We stitch relists back together and publish a per-region median True DOM. In Hamilton
> today that median is 67 days, across 2,268 active listings — the longest of the 15
> Ontario markets we track.
>
> TRREB reports average days-on-market monthly in Market Watch. The difference is
> relist-aware, per region, and refreshed nightly.
>
> Full table: pureproperty.ca/data/days-on-market
