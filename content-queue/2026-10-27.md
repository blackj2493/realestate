**Body:**

We pulled Whitby this morning and it's the softest board on the list we track today: **33% of active Whitby listings have cut their asking price**, with a typical reduction of 7.0%. That's across 467 active listings.

We're PureProperty, an Ontario housing-data site run by a licensed Ontario realtor.

Why this is easy to miss: a home relisted lower still prints as a single active listing at its new number, so the *share of sellers who've already blinked* gets averaged out of any headline price. We count the share of active inventory sitting below its own first ask, refreshed nightly, per region.

TRREB publishes average and benchmark prices monthly, and portals like HouseSigma show the price history on each individual listing. The angle here is a different cut of it: the region-wide share of active listings below their own first ask, updated every night.

Caveats: this is a share of *active* listings, not sold data — it counts sellers who've already dropped, not where deals actually land. And a seller who simply started high in a slow month looks the same here as genuine distress.
