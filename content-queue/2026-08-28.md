Median true days-on-market for active Richmond Hill listings is 72 days right now — the longest of the markets I track today (n=1,031 active).

"True" is doing the work in that sentence. Two active markets can report wildly different "days on market" for houses that have sat exactly as long. The difference is usually the counter, not the market.

Median true days-on-market for active Richmond Hill listings is now 72 days — the longest of the Ontario markets I track.

"True" is the whole point. When a listing is terminated and relisted, the portal DOM resets to zero. I stitch the relist chain back together and count from the first appearance. That's why my DOM runs higher than the portal number for the same homes. It isn't a different market; it's the same market with the clock left running.

Full market table, updated nightly: https://www.pureproperty.ca/data/days-on-market
