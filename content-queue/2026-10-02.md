```
Median true days-on-market for active Hamilton listings is now 69 days — the longest of the markets I track. That's the median across 2,313 active listings.

The word doing the work is "true." The DOM you see on a portal resets to zero every time a listing is cancelled and relisted, so a place that's been available since spring can show "6 days." So the surprise isn't just that Hamilton is slow; it's that the portal DOM for the same market is almost certainly telling you a shorter, friendlier story.

Where I might be wrong: if you think relist-stitching over-counts (e.g. a genuine price repositioning that should reset the clock), I want to hear the case. AMA on how the stitching works.

Figure and method: https://www.pureproperty.ca/data/days-on-market
```
