> listings is 67 days, across 2,849 listings. That's the longest of the 15 Ontario markets

> median at the city level and refresh it nightly, so the difference between "listed 4 days
> ago" and "has actually been for sale since July" is a column, not a hunch.
>
> 67 days isn't a crash signal on its own — it's one metric, and list-side only. But it's a
> long way from the "everything sells in a weekend" story Mississauga still carries.
>
> I'm a licensed Ontario realtor and I built PureProperty — a free data tool — because the
> board sits on numbers like this and the consumer sites won't show them.
