## Angle 1 — Whitby: 3.1 months of supply

- **Figure (verbatim):** `3.1 months of supply`
- **Region:** Whitby
- **Sample:** n = 450
- **Source URL:** https://www.pureproperty.ca/data/market-temperature
- **Why surprising:** Least inventory of the 15 markets tracked today. ≈4–6
  months is balanced; below that tilts hard toward sellers — the opposite of
  the "everything's cooling" read that the price-cut headlines invite.

### (a) Reddit — r/TorontoRealEstate

**Title:** Whitby is the tightest market I tracked today — 3.1 months of supply, while half the GTA is cutting prices

**Body:**
```
I run a nightly pull of active-listing and sales-pace data across 15 Ontario markets. Today Whitby came out at 3.1 months of supply — the lowest of the group.

For context, roughly 4–6 months of supply is considered balanced. Below that, the market structurally favours sellers, because listings are getting absorbed faster than they're being replaced. Whitby at 3.1 is well under that line.

What makes it worth flagging: it runs against the "buyers finally have leverage" narrative you see when a third of listings in nearby markets are cutting their asking price. Leverage clearly isn't evenly spread — it's a town-by-town thing right now, not a region-wide thing.

Sample is 450 active listings. Months of supply is a snapshot of current active inventory against the recent sales pace, so it moves — it's a temperature read, not a forecast.

I'm a licensed Ontario realtor and I built PureProperty — a free data tool — because the board sits on numbers like this and the consumer sites won't show them.

TRREB publishes months-of-inventory monthly at the board level; the difference here is nightly cadence and a per-market breakout. Tracker's here if you want to pull your own market: https://www.pureproperty.ca/data/market-temperature

AMA, or tell me where I'm reading this wrong — if you're on the ground in Whitby, does 3.1 match what you're seeing?
```

For a buyer, the read isn't "everything's on sale," it's "which of these cuts are stale-and-motivated vs freshly repriced-to-clear." A cut that's sat for weeks after the reduction is a different conversation than one that just dropped.

Sample is 1,983 active listings. This is cut *share* (has it been reduced at all), not total discount off some fair value — a listing can cut 8% and still be over-asking for the street.

Price history exists on the big portals per listing; the difference here is the market-wide share, updated nightly, so you can see how many sellers are cutting, not just whether one did: https://www.pureproperty.ca/data/price-cuts
