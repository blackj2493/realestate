# The 96% wall: a stale listing does not sell for less than its current ask

## Figures (verbatim)

GTA sales (Toronto + 13 GTA cities; condo apartments, detached, semi, freehold town),
deals signed Apr – Sep 2026, n = 28,365. "True days" = days from the first listing in an
unbroken chain of SALE listings (relists within 60 days count as the same campaign) to
the signed deal. "First ask" = the asking price at the start of that chain.

Condo apartments (n = 8,408):

| True days on market | Sales | Relisted | Sold vs current ask | Sold vs FIRST ask | Over asking |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0–7 | 508 | 0% | 98.7% | 98.6% | 26% |
| 8–14 | 978 | 1% | 98.0% | 98.1% | 21% |
| 15–30 | 1,909 | 3% | 97.1% | 96.9% | 15% |
| 31–60 | 2,047 | 15% | 96.2% | 94.7% | 8% |
| 61–90 | 1,163 | 35% | 95.9% | 92.0% | 7% |
| 91–180 | 1,133 | 64% | 96.2% | 88.9% | 8% |
| 181+ | 670 | 92% | 95.8% | 82.9% | 7% |

Houses (n = 19,957):

| True days on market | Sales | Relisted | Sold vs current ask | Sold vs FIRST ask | Over asking |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0–7 | 2,971 | 0% | 100.8% | 100.9% | 57% |
| 8–14 | 3,641 | 1% | 99.1% | 99.2% | 39% |
| 15–30 | 4,361 | 11% | 97.1% | 96.9% | 19% |
| 31–60 | 3,915 | 33% | 96.3% | 94.0% | 9% |
| 61–90 | 2,015 | 56% | 96.2% | 91.6% | 11% |
| 91–180 | 1,943 | 77% | 96.2% | 89.0% | 13% |
| 181+ | 1,111 | 94% | 95.7% | 81.8% | 12% |

- The headline: after 30 days, homes sell for about 96% of the CURRENT asking price,
  whether they sat 31 days or 181+. The gap against the FIRST asking price keeps growing
  (houses: 94.0% → 81.8%). The discount comes from the price cuts before the offer, not
  from the offer itself.
- 26.9% of these sales had been relisted at least once. For relisted homes, the board's
  day count showed a median of 23 days; the true count was 92 days.
- Same pattern in Toronto and in the 905, and in Apr–Sep 2025.

## Caveats

- Medians; the mix of homes differs between rows.
- Listing history in our data starts in 2024, so the longest chains can be cut short.
- A relist gap over 60 days starts a new campaign in this count.
- Sold data only: homes that never sold are not here.

## Prior art

TRREB and many sites publish average sale-to-list ratio and days on market. The
difference here: sale price against the FIRST asking price, by true days on market
across relists.

## Website callback

`/data/days-on-market` shows true days on market (relists joined up) by area. The post
should invite readers to check the true count for their area before they read a
listing's day count at face value.
