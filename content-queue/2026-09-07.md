**Title:** The typical active Richmond Hill listing has now sat 78 days (true DOM, n≈986)

**Body:**

>

> Tracker: https://www.pureproperty.ca/data/days-on-market
>
> AMA — or tell me where the relist-stitching could be over- or under-counting.

### (d) LinkedIn — **Type C (The method)**

> Richmond Hill's typical active listing has sat 78 days. The number most tools would show you
> is lower — and the gap is a measurement artefact, not a market signal.
>
> When a listing is terminated and re-entered, the MLS days-on-market field generally resets to
> zero. A property that has been available for four months can present as four days old. Any
> ranking built on the raw field inherits that reset.
>
> We stitch the relists back to the original list date, so 78 days is time the home has actually
> been on the market — the longest of the 15 Ontario markets in tonight's run (n≈986). The
> boards and the major portals all publish days-on-market; the difference here is what the clock
> is counting.
