### (a) Reddit — r/TorontoRealEstate

**Title:** The typical active Richmond Hill listing has now sat 103 days — longest true DOM of the markets I track

**Body:**
Median true days-on-market for active Richmond Hill listings is now 103 days — the longest of any market I'm currently tracking (n=1,098).

I say "true" DOM deliberately. The number most portals show you gets reset every time a listing is terminated and re-listed — so a place that's been sitting since spring can show up looking fresh. When you stitch the history back together, the typical Richmond Hill listing has been on the market for over three months. That's a carrying-cost story for the seller and a leverage story for the buyer.

If you're shopping here, the practical move is to sort by real time-on-market, not the reset date, and open conversations with the ones deepest underwater on carry.

Disclosure: I'm a licensed Ontario realtor and I built PureProperty. The DOM tracker is here: https://www.pureproperty.ca/data/days-on-market

AMA / tell me where I'm wrong — if you think re-list resets are legitimate (new price, new strategy) and true DOM overstates staleness, I want to hear it.
