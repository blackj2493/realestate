**Title:** Everyone's talking about the soft GTA market — but Burlington is sitting at 3.3 months of supply, the tightest of the 15 Ontario markets I track

**Body:**

We're PureProperty, an Ontario housing-data site run by a licensed Ontario realtor.

Pulled months-of-supply across 15 Ontario markets this morning. The headlines are all soft
market right now — and most of the board agrees. But **Burlington is sitting at 3.3 months
of supply**, the least inventory of the 15 markets we track today.

Months of supply = how long it would take to clear every active listing at the current
sales pace. Roughly 4–6 months is considered balanced; below that tilts toward sellers.
So at 3.3, Burlington is on the seller side of the line while a lot of its neighbours are
not. Sample is 739 active listings.

Caveats: this is one morning's snapshot, it's a city-wide average (one neighbourhood can
run hot or cold against it), and months-of-supply moves fast as new listings land. TRREB
publishes months of inventory at the board level monthly; we compute it per-municipality
off active and sold counts and refresh it nightly, which is the only reason a single city
stands out from the board here.

What does the supply picture look like for your city? Full 15-market table, updated nightly:
https://www.pureproperty.ca/data/market-temperature?utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=burlington-supply

Is this matching what you're seeing on the ground in Burlington — still competitive, or has it loosened since the summer?
