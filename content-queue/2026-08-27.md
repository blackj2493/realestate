**Body:**
```
Ran the rent-vs-buy numbers this morning: an Ottawa 1-bedroom grosses about 7.0% gross yield (n=4,008), the best of the Ontario markets I track today.

"Gross" is doing real work in that sentence — it's annual rent over price, before financing, vacancy, maintenance, condo fees or taxes. Net is a very different and much smaller number once carrying costs go in. So this isn't "7% in your pocket"; it's the top-line ratio, which is the number that decides whether a unit is even worth underwriting.

Door Insight publishes Toronto house rents monthly, so rent data itself isn't hidden; what I'm putting next to it is the price side, as a live gross-yield ratio per market, refreshed nightly, so you can see where the rent-to-price math is least bad.
```
