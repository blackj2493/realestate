### (a) Reddit — r/TorontoRealEstate

**Title:** The typical active Richmond Hill listing has now sat 79 days — longest true DOM of the markets I track (n≈1,100)

**Body:**

> Current numbers from a free tracker I run: the **typical active Richmond Hill listing
> has now sat 79 days** (median), across about 1,100 active listings. That's the longest
> median days-on-market of the Ontario markets I track today.
>
> The catch with DOM is that the clock often resets. Terminate-and-relist restarts the
> counter, so the number a portal shows you can be much younger than how long the home
> has actually been trying to sell. I stitch the history so the DOM reflects the true
> continuous marketing time rather than the latest relist. TRREB reports average DOM
> monthly at the board level; the per-municipality median on the *true* clock is what I
> compute nightly.
>
> I'm a licensed Ontario realtor and I built PureProperty — a free data tool — because
> the board sits on numbers like this and the consumer sites won't show them.
>
> AMA / tell me where I'm wrong: if you're an agent working Richmond Hill, does 79 days
> median feel right, or is the detached-vs-condo mix skewing it?
