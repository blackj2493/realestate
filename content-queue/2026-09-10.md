**Draft (Type C):**

> "Days on market" is the most quoted number in residential real estate, and one of the easiest to game.
>
> When a listing is cancelled and re-entered under a new MLS number, the board's day count starts over at zero. A home that's been for sale since spring can read as freshly listed. That single mechanic quietly flatters slow markets.
>
> Stitch the listing history back together so the clock survives the relist, and the picture changes. On that basis the typical active Oakville listing has now sat 77 days — the longest of the Ontario markets we track. HouseSigma also surfaces relist history; what we add is a per-market median of true DOM, refreshed nightly.
>
> A median of active, still-unsold listings isn't a sold-time figure — but it's the honest denominator, and it's the one that resets least when you're not watching.
>
> Full table: pureproperty.ca/data/days-on-market
