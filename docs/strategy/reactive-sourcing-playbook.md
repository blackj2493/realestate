# Reactive sourcing — responding to journalist requests

**Why this is first, ahead of every cold pitch.** On a reactive platform the journalist has
already stated the need and set the deadline. You are not persuading anyone that a story
exists — you are answering a question someone asked in public. Conversion is far higher than
cold outreach, credit is near-automatic because they solicited the material, and **none of it
depends on having a scoop.** That matters here: we do not have one. See
`data-outreach-ready-to-send.md` for the prior-art table showing why.

## Where to sign up

| platform | notes |
|---|---|
| **Qwoted** | Best signal-to-noise for finance/real-estate. Free for sources. Set alerts for `real estate`, `housing`, `mortgage`, `rent`, `Toronto`, `Canada`. |
| **Help a B2B Writer** | Twice-daily digest, low competition, heavily used by trade press and finance blogs — these produce **dofollow** links more often than news sites. |
| **SourceBottle** | Weaker for Canada but free; the odd local hit. |
| **#JournoRequest** on X / Bluesky | Unstructured, fast. Search `journorequest housing`, `journorequest real estate`, and `PRrequest`. |
| **Reddit** r/CanadaHousing, r/TorontoRealEstate | Not a pitch channel. Answer questions with a number and a link; see [[reddit-opportunity-monitor]]. |

Also worth watching: reporters posting "anyone got data on…" directly. Answer those the same way.

## The rules

1. **Answer within an hour of seeing it, or don't bother.** Reactive queries are won on speed.
   The first credible response usually takes it. If the deadline has passed, skip it.
2. **Answer the question asked.** Do not redirect to the number you'd rather talk about.
3. **Lead with the number, not with who you are.** They are scanning dozens of replies.
4. **Give the figure in the email itself.** Never make them click to find out whether you have
   what they need. The link is for sourcing and for the credit — not for the answer.
5. **Include the caveat yourself.** It is the single strongest trust signal available and it
   costs nothing. It is also what stops you being quoted wrongly.
6. **Never quote absolute sales counts.** VOW subset, runs 15–23% low. Prices, days on market,
   price cuts, fees and ratios are all fine — see [[metrics-vs-official-validation]].
7. **Never claim novelty.** If a board already publishes something similar, say so and say
   what's different. Being the honest one is the whole advantage.
8. **One follow-up, never two.** If they don't reply, they went another way. Let it go.

## Template A — you have exactly what they asked for

> **Subject:** Re: [their query subject] — Ontario numbers, nightly
>
> Hi [name],
>
> [DIRECT ANSWER IN THE FIRST SENTENCE. e.g. "In Toronto, 22.0% of active listings have cut
> their asking price; Hamilton is highest of the markets we cover at 26.4%."]
>
> [ONE SENTENCE OF CONTEXT THEY CAN USE. e.g. "Broken out by neighbourhood, it ranges from
> X% in [area] to Y% in [area]."]
>
> It's free and public at [tracker URL], updated nightly from MLS® data with the method
> written out. Happy to be quoted, or to stay on background if you'd rather.
>
> One caveat so you don't get caught: our feed is a large subset of the market rather than
> all of it, so I won't give you transaction counts — for volumes you want TRREB or CREA.
> Proportions and medians like the above are solid.
>
> If you need a specific neighbourhood, property type or price band, tell me and I'll pull it
> today.
>
> [NAME], PureProperty.ca
> [PHONE] · pureproperty.ca/data/for-journalists

## Template B — you have something adjacent, not the exact thing

Do not stretch. Say what you have, say what you don't, and offer the pull.

> Hi [name],
>
> I don't have [exact thing] — but I can get you [closest real thing] by [time], if that's
> useful for the piece.
>
> For what it's worth on [their topic]: [one genuinely relevant number + source URL].
>
> If it's not what you need, no problem — happy to point you at TRREB/CMHC for [the thing
> they actually want], which is where I'd go for it myself.
>
> [NAME], PureProperty.ca

**The referral in that last line is not wasted.** Being the source who sends a reporter
somewhere better is exactly why they come back to you next time.

## Template C — they want a quote, not data

Only use if you're willing to be named. A quote almost always carries a link, and it is the
one thing WOWA, Zolo and the portals structurally cannot supply — there is nobody to call.

> Hi [name],
>
> Happy to be quoted. On [topic], attributable to [NAME], [TITLE], PureProperty.ca:
>
> "[ONE OR TWO SENTENCES. Concrete, tied to a number you can defend, no adjectives.]"
>
> The number behind that is at [URL] if you want to check it or chart it. I can also give
> you the neighbourhood-level breakdown if the piece needs local specificity.
>
> Reachable at [PHONE] until [time] if an editor has questions.

## Template D — the custom pull, delivered

The moment the credit is won or lost. Attach the numbers, state the citation, make crediting
easier than not crediting.

> Hi [name],
>
> Here's the [X] cut you asked for:
>
> [SMALL TABLE, PASTED INLINE. Five to ten rows, not fifty. Include the sample size per row.]
>
> Method: [one line — what's counted, what's excluded, what date range].
> [Flag any row too thin to publish, explicitly.]
>
> Source line if you use it:
> "Source: PureProperty.ca" linking to [tracker URL]
>
> There's a live embeddable version of this chart at [embed URL] if you'd rather it stayed
> current after publication — one line of HTML, and it updates itself.
>
> [NAME], PureProperty.ca

## Logging

Log every response: date, platform, outlet, journalist, what was asked, what was sent,
whether it ran, whether it linked. Two reasons — you will otherwise double-respond to the
same person within a month, and you cannot tell which platform is worth the time without it.

Judge the channels separately: **trade and finance blogs deliver dofollow backlinks; major
news outlets deliver publicity and often a `nofollow`.** Both are worth having. Do not
conclude a channel failed because it produced the wrong one.
