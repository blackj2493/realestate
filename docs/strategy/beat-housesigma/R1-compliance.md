# R1 — Compliance / TRREB Guardrail (cross-examination + rulings)

**Author:** `compliance` | **Round:** 1 | Read all six peer R0s. This file issues the binding SAFE / GATED / FORBIDDEN rulings the lead requested, each stamped with the clause. Where a peer's framing is wrong, I correct it by name.

> **Doctrine recap (from R0, the test I apply to everything below):** Two feeds, two rulebooks. **VOW §6.2(f)** is the *only* sentence in either agreement that permits derived analytics, and it carries **two inseparable qualifiers** — derivative tools are allowed **(a) "on their VOW(s)"** [= secure, password-protected, VOW §3.2] **AND (b) "solely for the purpose of providing residential real estate brokerage services"** to a **bona-fide Consumer** [§3.2 Purpose]. The **IDX feed has NO analytics carve-out at all** (IDX §6.2(f) flatly bars derivatives/merges). So the governing question for every idea is: *is this output derived from VOW data, and if so, does it stay behind the gate AND serve a bona-fide consumer?*

## The five rulings the lead asked for

### RULING 1 — perf-arch's uncapped COUNT-ONLY heat/yield/DOM aggregate layer beneath the ≤100 pins
**Verdict: SAFE for active (IDX) aggregates · GATED for sold/VOW-derived aggregates. Two-tier, not one ruling.**

- **The 100-cap question:** A **count/density/heat statistic is NOT a "Listing viewed or retrieved,"** so it is **outside the §6.3(b) 100-cap.** §6.3(b) limits *"the number of Listing(s) that Consumers may view or retrieve... in response to an inquiry"* — a choropleth cell labelled "47 actives, median DOM 31" discloses no Listing. **perf-arch is correct that aggregate ≠ listing display.** SAFE on that axis. *(IDX/VOW §6.3(b).)*
- **The gating question (the part perf-arch flagged to me):** it splits by data source.
  - **Heat built from the ACTIVE (IDX) index** (count, active-DOM, price-vs-list compression, active inventory density): **SAFE and may be PUBLIC.** Active display is the licensed Purpose; an aggregate of it is even further from a Listing. *(IDX §3.2 Purpose + §6.3(b).)*
  - **Heat built from SOLD/VOW data** (sold-price intensity, realized-yield-from-sold-rent, sold-DOM, sold $/sqft per cell): this is a **derivative of VOW Listing Information → §6.2(f) applies → it must live behind the gate.** An aggregate doesn't launder the source: §6.2(f) governs *"derivative analytical tools or reports,"* and a sold-yield heatmap is exactly that. **GATED — `requireConsumer`, not public.** *(VOW §6.2(f) both qualifiers.)*
- **The hard guardrail for perf-arch:** the cells must render as **aggregates only**. The moment a cell is small enough to identify a single transaction (a rural cell with one sold, a draw-polygon over one property), an "aggregate" silently becomes a disclosed VOW Listing → §6.3(b)+§6.2(f) breach. **Apply a minimum-N suppression (e.g. hide/blur any sold cell with N < 5)** — the same logic already protecting `bubbles/stats.ts` `MEDIAN_SAMPLE_CAP` server-side. This is a *new* requirement I'm imposing on the move, not a veto of it.

### RULING 2 — growth's programmatic "Investor Lens" SEO pages + weekly report from `region_aggregates` (active-listing aggregates only)
**Verdict: SAFE for public/SEO — *conditionally*, and the conditions are not optional.**

This is the one big public/SEO play I affirmatively bless, because it's the rare lane that's both high-traffic and clearly inside the box. But "active aggregates only" is doing enormous load-bearing work, so I'm pinning the conditions:
1. **Active-IDX aggregates only. Zero VOW.** No sold price, no sold-derived median, no per-property AVM, no realized cap rate (which is VOW-rent-derived) on any indexable page or its `<meta>`/OG tags. The instant a "region cap rate" is computed from sold or leased VOW records, the page becomes a public VOW derivative → **FORBIDDEN** (§6.2(f) qualifier (a)). *Verify with `data-quant` exactly which columns of `region_aggregates` are sold/VOW-sourced — I suspect some are; those are off the public page.*
2. **No per-listing enumeration.** A "city investment" page may show distributions/counts, but must not become a browsable list of >100 listings (§6.3(b)) and must not deep-link a wall of sold records.
3. **Brokerage + notices.** Any page that surfaces even one *active Listing* (e.g. "top 5 actives by compression") carries brokerage at sibling weight (§6.3(c)) and the §6.3(i) "deemed reliable / not guaranteed by PROPTX" + §6.3(k) bona-fide-consumer notices.
4. **No LLM in the copy pipeline.** The report/page narrative may **not** be generated by passing IDX/VOW fields to an LLM (§6.2(k) / §4). Template + deterministic fills only. *(This bites growth's "weekly report" — the prose must be templated, not AI-written from listing data.)*
5. **The report is email/active-aggregate; it must not embed gated VOW numbers** that then sit in an inbox/forwarded thread outside the gate.

*Rule basis: IDX §3.2 Purpose (active public display licensed), §6.3(b)/(c)/(i)/(k), §6.2(k)/§4.*

### RULING 3 — growth's "Deal Card" OG/share export of a user's underwriting/AVM analysis to BiggerPockets etc.
**Verdict: SPLIT. The active-deterministic version is SAFE-GATED-EXPORT. The AVM/VOW-derived version is FORBIDDEN to export publicly.**

This is the single most dangerous idea in the whole council and I'm ruling on it precisely, because growth has (smartly) pre-split it.
- **Deal Card containing only ACTIVE-IDX deterministic metrics** — list price, active True-DOM-on-the-current-listing, price-vs-list compression, carrying cost, **cash-on-cash computed from the user's OWN inputs** (their rate/down/rent assumption is *their* number, not VOW): **SAFE to export**, with two conditions — (i) brokerage displayed on the card (§6.3(c)), (ii) the card is a *single property* the sharer is analyzing (a card ≠ a >100 listing feed, §6.3(b)). This is the version of Move 2's loop that survives.
- **Deal Card containing AVM estimate, Value-Add upside, sold comps, sold price history, or realized (VOW-rent) yield**: **FORBIDDEN to export to a logged-out recipient / external site.** This is the crux growth asked me: *"does exporting gated §6.2(f) derivative output outside the password gate break it?"* — **Yes, categorically.** §6.2(f)'s permission is expressly conditioned on the derivative living **"on their VOW(s)"** (gated) and serving a **bona-fide consumer**. A BiggerPockets reader is neither inside the VOW nor a consumer we've established a relationship with. Posting an AVM/upside number there is **publication/syndication of a VOW derivative to the public** → triggers **§6.2(f) (lost both qualifiers) + §6.2(r)/(s) (syndicate/redistribute) + Purpose §3.2 (non-consumer)**. It is functionally identical to the public-AVM tool I pre-vetoed in R0 — just user-triggered, which does not cure it (the AVP/Member is *"fully and directly liable for any breach,"* IDX/VOW §11.1; "a user did it" is not a defence).
- **My yes-and so the loop still works:** the public card shows the *active-deterministic* verdict + an aggregate teaser ("comps suggest upside — see the full AVM breakdown inside"), and the **AVM/upside number unlocks only when the recipient lands behind the gate.** Same pattern as the sold-teaser already shipped in `sold/route.ts`. The loop keeps its virality; the regulated number stays home.

### RULING 4 — data-quant/product-ux exposing AVM per-feature breakdown + sold-price timeline (public teaser vs gated full)
**Verdict: The full breakdown + timeline are GATED (consumer + terms). A non-numeric / aggregate teaser is SAFE public.**

- **Full `AVMAdjustmentBreakdown` ("$X = anchor $Y +$Z bath −$W lot") and the property's sold-price timeline (2019 $640k → 2023 $880k):** **GATED.** Both are VOW-sold-derivatives → §6.2(f) → behind `requireConsumer` with terms enforced. data-quant's R0 already proposes gating these — **I confirm that is correct and required, not optional.** The timeline in particular is raw VOW sold *values* (close prices/dates), the most sensitive VOW field; it must never reach an anon client or a crawler. *(VOW §6.2(f), §3.2 Purpose; this is the `property_sale_history` close_price data.)*
- **The teaser that's allowed public:** "We can show you a glass-box estimate and this home's full sold history — sign in" (capability statement, **no numbers**), or an *aggregate* shape ("3 prior sales on record"). A **count** of prior sales is borderline-OK (it's a count, not a Listing), but **any close-price/sold-date value is gated.** When in doubt on a single-property count, gate it — single-property counts edge toward identifying a transaction.
- **Operational note for data-quant:** even gated, the breakdown must stay **deterministic** (it is — anchor-and-adjust, no LLM, §4-clean per your R0). Don't let a future "explain this estimate in plain English" feature route the breakdown through an LLM — that would convert a §4-clean feature into a §6.2(k) breach.

### RULING 5 (the lead's confirm) — does the public active-listing terminal (growth's funnel-flip) sit inside IDX rules cleanly?
**Verdict: YES — SAFE, and it is *already the shipped state*. Flip the homepage. With four conditions.**

growth is factually right that `/properties` is already public + indexable (`robots.ts` allows it, `sitemap.ts` priority 0.9) and the VOW gate already hard-blocks sold/AVM for anon (`sold/route.ts` count-only teaser; `requireConsumer.ts`). Dropping anon users straight into the **active** terminal is licensed use (IDX §3.2 Purpose). **I confirm Move 1's compliance flag #1: there is no VOW leakage in the public terminal path** *as currently architected* — sold/AVM is server-gated. Conditions to keep it clean when traffic scales:
1. **Per-query result set stays ≤100** for the anon terminal (§6.3(b)) — already `MAX_LISTINGS = 100`; infinite-scroll/"load more" must not let one inquiry retrieve the 101st (perf-arch's heat layer is the right way to "show more" without breaking this).
2. **Brokerage on every anon-visible surface** — card, **map popup**, drawer, compare cell (§6.3(c)). *This is my #2 hard limit from R0 and it is NOT yet audited on the map popup / compare / ledger heart — see challenge to product-ux below.*
3. **§6.3(i) + §6.3(k) notices present** on the public terminal (deemed-reliable + bona-fide-consumer). Verify they render for anon, not just signed-in.
4. **No active-only derived metric leaks the IDX no-carve-out rule.** Subtle but real: a deterministic metric shown on an anon listing must be sourced so it inherits a lawful basis. True DOM, sold comps, AVM = VOW-derived = gated (fine, already gated). **Active-listing-only computed metrics (e.g. "price vs its own list history," carrying cost) are OK to show** because they're not "derivative analytical tools" merging the dataset — they're a reformat/computation on the single active listing's own fields (§6.3(f) permits field-selection display; a per-listing carrying-cost off list price + tax is a display computation, not a dataset derivative). **But anything that aggregates the active dataset into a new analytic (e.g. "this is cheaper than 80% of the neighbourhood") leans on IDX §6.2(f) which has no carve-out — keep those as aggregates/counts (Ruling 1) or gate them.** This is the trap nobody else has spotted: *active data is public, but active-data ANALYTICS still hit IDX §6.2(f).*

## Direct challenges to peers (named)

**→ `growth` (my assigned primary challenge — you have the most ideas to rule on, and the most revocation exposure):**
Your Move 2 framing — *"Compliance-safe because the card shows derived deterministic metrics... but the AVM/sold-derived numbers need a ruling"* — has the risk exactly inverted. **"Deterministic" is not the safe-making property; "active-sourced AND gated-if-VOW" is.** A *deterministic* AVM number is still a VOW derivative and still forbidden to publish (§6.2(f)) — determinism only satisfies §4/§6.2(k) (the no-LLM rule), it does nothing for the publication/syndication rules. Please internalize the two-axis test: **(axis 1) is it VOW-derived?** → if yes it's gated; **(axis 2) was an LLM involved?** → if yes it's forbidden regardless. A feature must pass *both*. Your Deal Card export passes axis 2 (deterministic) but **fails axis 1** the moment it carries AVM/upside/sold numbers to BiggerPockets. Ruling 3 gives you the loop that survives — take the active-deterministic card + aggregate teaser, not the AVM card. Second: your weekly report's **prose cannot be LLM-written from listing data** (§6.2(k)) — I need you to concede the report is templated, not generative, or it's a §4 breach.

**→ `competitive` and `persona`:** we are aligned and you've already done my job for me on the public-AVM veto — thank you. One precision for `competitive`'s Move 2 headline (*"HouseSigma's estimate can't tell a reno from a gut job — ours can"*): the **marketing claim is fine; the tool it points to must sit behind the gate.** You can *advertise* the condition-aware AVM publicly (a claim is not VOW data); you cannot *show the number* publicly (§6.2(f)). Keep the bait public, the catch gated.

**→ `data-quant`:** your Move 1 (build `rental_market_index` from the IDX **for-rent** feed + **leased** records in `raw_vow_sold`) — I bless the architecture but flag a sourcing line: **rent aggregates built from IDX active-rentals are IDX-class; rent aggregates built from VOW leased records are VOW-class.** If the resulting per-property cap rate/yield is *materially* VOW-leased-derived, then that cap-rate number is a **VOW derivative → gated** (cannot be a public SEO column, ties back to Ruling 2 condition #1). Cleanest path: if you want yield numbers usable on **public** pages, derive the rent model from the **IDX for-rent feed only**; reserve the VOW-leased blend for the **gated** terminal/AVM. Tell me the blend and I'll tell you the surface.

**→ `perf-arch`:** beyond Ruling 1's min-N suppression — your Move 2 (ISR-cache the listing detail page) is *good for compliance too* (cached active-IDX page = fine), **but the cache must be auth-partitioned**: the VOW-gated fields (AVM, sold history, breakdown) must **never** be baked into the shared ISR HTML or a CDN cache, or you'd serve a gated VOW derivative to an anon/crawler from cache (§6.2(f)). Cache the IDX body; compute the VOW rail per-request behind `requireConsumer`. You said as much ("VOW-gated fields stay computed at the edge per-auth") — I'm making it a hard condition, not an aside.
