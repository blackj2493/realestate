# Move 2 — Warm-well + named-B2B-buyer enumeration

**Goal:** convert "there are ~hundreds of flippers" (an aspiration) into a **named, countable list** of
people you can actually reach this quarter — and explicitly name the single B2B buyer the whole revenue
floor leans on. 100% controllable. This is the move that tells you, honestly, whether a circle and a
recurring dollar line can even form from your real network.

**Time:** one evening. Be honest — a name only counts if you could text them and they'd reply.

## Why this is load-bearing
The win bar needs (a) an activated circle and (b) ONE recurring revenue line. Both draw from a **finite warm
well**. The prior plan's fatal flaw was multiplying an *uncounted* 300–600 by a conversion rate. This move
refuses that: you size the floor off the *observed* well, not a borrowed number. The single biggest residual
risk in the entire strategy is "the warm well yields zero recurring line" — this worksheet is how you look
that risk in the eye on day one.

## Output / decision
- A filled Worksheet A/B/C with **hard counts**.
- The viable-cockpit count → sets the (a) target.
- ≥1 credibly named, firewall-safe B2B buyer with a warm intro path → the (b) floor has a real primary path.
