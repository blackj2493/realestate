# Phase-Zero Validation Kit

> **Why this exists.** Three independent strategy deliberations (a prior committee, plus two multi-agent
> advisory exercises on 2026-06-23) converged on one hard truth: a *meaningful* win for PureProperty —
> new repeatable revenue + a defended position, not a day-job floor — cannot be certified at 97% from a
> chair, because it irreducibly requires **at least one external "yes"** (a paying buyer, a sponsor, or a
> Broker-of-Record). The winning strategy is the **GTA Flip Scoreboard + Private Cockpit** (founder-edge
> archetype — see `../2026-06-23-strategy-tournament-flip-scoreboard.md`). Its honest confidence settled at
> ~58/100, with the gap concentrated in three *uncontrollable* unknowns.
>
> **Phase Zero is the only thing that moves the number.** It is a cheap, mostly evening/weekend-doable
> sprint, carved OUT of the 18-month execution clock, that converts those unknowns into known facts BEFORE
> you commit. You spend almost nothing to learn whether the company thesis is real. If it isn't, you stop —
> and that is a win, not a loss.

## The win bar this validates (the "meaningful" definition)
Within ~18 months, on controllable output, ALL of:
- **(a) In-niche footprint** — an activated, VOW-compliant cockpit circle + 12 consecutive months of a canonical Scoreboard index (gated on *publication consistency*, not reach).
- **(b) New repeatable revenue** — ONE recurring, audience-independent dollar line (primary: a single named lender/brokerage paying for a neutral vault-backed data report).
- **(c) A durable compounding asset** — an irreversible, license-on-the-line graded track record a richer incumbent can't back-date and a poorer local can't reproduce.
- **(d) Zero legal exposure** — aggregate-only public surface with min-n; every cockpit member a documented EXP VOW client; raw VOW rows architecturally barred from any LLM.
- **(e) A gated "force" path** — "scorekeeper of GTA flip economics," reachable only as a race condition (pure upside; never assumed).

## Sequencing rule (protect the day job)
- **Moves 1–3 are evening/weekend work and run FIRST**, in parallel where possible. They answer the load-bearing questions cheaply.
- **Move 4 (the legal/BoR packet) is drafted on an evening but its *answer* depends on other people's calendars.** Send it EARLY so it runs in the background. Batch every ask into ONE document — do not drip-feed your Broker-of-Record.
- **The 18-month clock does not start until Move 4 clears.** Until then you are in the safe default posture: aggregate-only, non-branded, EXP-native.

**Decision logic:** if signals 1, 2, and 3 all land in Phase Zero → start the 18-month clock with high (but never 97%) confidence on a meaningful win. If any **hard-fails** → use the kill/pivot rule in that move's doc *before* spending a single growth hour.

## Kill gates (pre-decided, so you don't rationalize later)
- **Min-n (Move 1):** 0 gradeable submarkets even at city grain → the public-Scoreboard surface is not viable here; either change city or drop the public-index leg.
- **Warm well (Move 2):** < ~20 viable cockpit names AND no credible single B2B buyer → the circle + B2B floor can't form; do not proceed on the company thesis.
- **Activation (Move 3):** < ~40% unprompted return AND no observed preference-reversal → it's a list, not a circle; the data edge doesn't change behavior. Demote to a personal-productivity tool.
- **Legal (Move 4):** hard-NO on aggregate-VOW publishing → the public index collapses to circle-only; recompute the win without the public leg before continuing.
