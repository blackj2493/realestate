# Move 4 — The ONE batched legal / Broker-of-Record packet

**Goal:** get, in writing, the handful of yes/no answers that determine whether the public + branded version
of the strategy is even legal — and whether your revenue can flow cleanly. This is the **only existential
gate** in the kit. It is the one thing you don't control, so you (a) batch every question into a single
document, (b) send it **early** so it runs against other people's calendars while you do Moves 1–3, and (c)
design the strategy to **degrade gracefully** on any "no."

**Time:** drafting is one evening (template below). The answer is gatekeeper-dependent — that's the point of
sending it first.

> **The 18-month clock does not start until this clears.** Until then, operate in the safe default posture:
> aggregate-only public surface, no branded solicitation, EXP-native presentation, cockpit access limited to
> documented EXP VOW clients. Build the graded/branded layer DARK; unlock it only on a written YES.

## Who it goes to
- A **TRESA/RECO-literate real-estate lawyer** (this is a real four-figure outlay — budget it; it is the
  cheapest insurance you will ever buy against losing the license the whole product hangs on).
- Your **EXP Broker-of-Record** (and, where relevant, EXP franchise/PROPTX for URL + brand sign-off).

## The packet — questions to answer (batch ALL of these into one document)

### Section 1 — Publishing & data scope (unlocks the public Scoreboard)
1. **(1a) Aggregate-VOW publishing:** May we publish *aggregate counts and medians* derived from VOW sold data
   (no raw listings, enforced min-n) on a public surface? On what attribution/disclaimer terms?
2. **(1b) Branded publishing:** May that public surface carry the "PureProperty / GTA Flip Scoreboard" brand,
   or must it be presented as an EXP service / under EXP's name? *(If branded is a NO, we default to
   EXP-native — pre-decided.)*
3. **(1c) Forward graded predictions:** May a *registrant* publish dated, on-the-record predicted-value bands
   that are later graded against actual sales? On what disclosure terms? *(This is the durable moat; if NO,
   the track record stays private/circle-only.)*
4. **(1d) Forwardable artifact contents:** What may a shareable teardown/report contain (aggregate + methodology
   only?) without re-exposing gated VOW values?

### Section 2 — The cockpit & VOW client status
5. **(2a)** Confirm: every cockpit member must be a **documented EXP client with an executed VOW consumer
   agreement on file** for them to see gated sold/AVM data. (We will gate on this regardless.)
6. **(2b)** Is a logged-in prospective-consumer terminal + a licensed-agent paired teardown a blessed use?

### Section 3 — Money flow (unlocks the revenue rails)
7. **(3a) Referral / representation revenue:** Confirm all trade-connected remuneration flows through EXP, and
   the mechanism for it to reach you.
8. **(3b) The B2B neutral data report (the primary recurring line):** Will EXP **administer/invoice** revenue
   from a lender/brokerage paying for an aggregate, neutral data report? **If NO:** is a separate non-VOW,
   no-representation analytics entity outside RECO trade-remuneration scope a clean home for it? *(The B2B
   floor is a company candidate ONLY if 3b returns a clean path — this is the single most important money
   question in the kit.)*
9. **(3c) Sponsorship:** May a neutral (non-conflicted) sponsor underwrite the public index, and how must that
   be disclosed?

### Section 4 — Brand, conflict & content
10. **(4a) Trade-name / DBA:** May "PureProperty / [mark]" be registered/authorized under EXP, with the
    brokerage ID shown on every surface (anti-holding-out)?
11. **(4b) Accuracy-claim advertising:** Is a published quantified hit-rate (e.g. "graded 25 calls, here are the
    misses") permissible registrant advertising? On what disclosure terms?
12. **(4c) Referral-fee conflict disclosure:** Confirm the per-transaction written disclosure language (you may
    receive a fee / you are a licensed agent with a commercial interest) at the CTA/intake step.
13. **(4d) Uncompensated-intro:** Confirm an *uncompensated* warm-intro ask is outside RECO referral-remuneration
    rules (no cash, fee credit, or gift may attach to an intro).
14. **(4e) PROPTX URL slots:** How many of EXP's 3 PROPTX URLs are free, and will EXP commit one in writing to
    this surface?

## Branch logic (pre-decide the response to each outcome)
| Outcome | Pre-decided response |
|---|---|
| **YES on 1a/1b/1c + a URL slot (3b clean)** | Unlock the full public, branded, graded surface + the B2B report. Start the clock. |
| **SILENCE / slow** | Stay in the default posture; floor on CREA-DDF / public-aggregate surfaces + EXP-native cockpit; keep building the graded engine dark. |
| **NO on 1b (brand)** | Run EXP-native branding; the distinctive brand survives as a *non-trade* media/data property, not on the agenting surface. |
| **Hard NO on 1a (aggregate-VOW publishing)** | Public Scoreboard collapses to **circle-only**; recompute the win without the public leg before continuing. |
| **NO on 3b (EXP won't invoice B2B) + no clean entity** | Cut the B2B rail; the recurring line must come from sponsor or repeat-Playbook, or the floor magnitude shrinks — state it honestly. |

## Output / decision
- A signed/emailed written answer to each numbered question (file it).
- Proof signal #3 (written YES on aggregate-VOW + branded publishing) ticked or not.
- The branch you're in, and therefore which surfaces go live when the clock starts.
- **Do not market anything attributed, branded, or graded-on-the-record before this returns.**
