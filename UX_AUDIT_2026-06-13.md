## TL;DR — the one decision that unblocks everything

Both the prosecution and the defense — who were told to fight — **converged** on the same root problem: the product is **two products in one trench coat**. "Bloomberg Terminal for institutional analysts" (the aesthetic) vs. "lead magnet for a solo, licence-monetized Brampton-flipper referral business" (the actual business). Until you **explicitly pick the flipper/investor lead-gen north star** (which your own business memo already implies), every roadmap decision stays incoherent and half your backlog can't be prioritized.

The contradiction stated plainly: you cannot simultaneously want **Realtor.ca-scale traffic** AND **filter out 99% of visitors**. Pick one. For a referral business, lead *quality × volume of qualified leads* is the revenue lever — not raw traffic, and not manufactured scarcity.

**Where they agreed (your 95%-certainty zone):**
1. Fix the credibility bugs first — they attack the "trust the math" moat directly.
2. **Kill the false "most applications are declined / manually verified" copy immediately** — it's false-in-code and a RECO professional-conduct / misrepresentation risk. *Unanimous, non-negotiable.*
3. One velvet rope, harvested once: keep intent capture, make the final step the magic-link, and **route the captured intent to you**.
4. Show-then-gate: let cold users into the terminal; gate the *shadow data*, not the door.
5. Make a dense, shadow-data-per-row ledger the differentiator (photos stay — as a toggle or alongside).
6. One coherent identity ritual.

**Where they diverged (genuine open questions for you):** dense table as *default* (defender) vs *opt-in toggle* (prosecutor); keep *3* intent questions (defender) vs collapse to *1* (prosecutor); whether a homeowner-value funnel belongs at all.

## Triaged actions (only the still-live items)

### Fix this week (credibility + the lie)
- **Kill the false-scarcity copy** — `apply/page.tsx:565` ("Most applications are declined") and `page.tsx:66` ("All accounts are manually verified"). It's false in code and a RECO/misrepresentation risk. Replace with truthful exclusivity ("Built for serious investors — not browsers"). *Both adversaries: unanimous.*
- **C1 — move the dark theme to `<body>`** so `/avm`, `/hidden-equity` (and any interior route) stop rendering white-on-white. High blast radius, cheap fix. Broken-white pages read as "abandoned."
- **C2 — gate the underwriting sandbox by property type.** Pass `PropertySubType` into `UnderwritingSandbox`; for vacant land / non-rentable types, suppress the rent/cashflow/yield block (show structural underwrite or nothing) rather than seeding a fabricated rent. This is the single most credibility-lethal bug for your one persona that matters.
- **C4 — mobile fallback** so prices never truncate and the logo doesn't overlap the persona control. A card fallback is enough; a perfect responsive table is not required. Brampton flipper traffic skews mobile.

### Fix eventually
- **T3 — ship the dense, shadow-data-per-row ledger** (True DOM, price compression, est. cap/yield as scannable badges). Both sides agree this is the clearest "measurably better than HouseSigma" surface. *Open question: default view (defender) vs opt-in toggle (prosecutor).*

### Ignore for now (defer)
- **C8 — keyboard/focus a11y on ledger rows.** Correct in the abstract; zero referral dollars this quarter. Roll in when the row component is rebuilt for T3 anyway.

## Strategic tensions — verdicts (consensus + divergence)

- **T1 Velvet rope — REFINE, don't amputate.** Collapse the redundant *second* login (the third application step **is** the magic-link account); keep a high-signal intent question and **route the answer to you** as a lead. Both sides agree the double wall is waste; they split on 1 vs 3 questions. *Confidence ~85%.*
- **T2 Zero-TTV — add "Explore the Terminal"; gate shadow data, not the door.** Anon users fly the dense map+ledger with locked-teaser shadow metrics (already your VOW model). This *honors* the spec's own Zero-TTV principle. *Confidence ~90%.*
- **T3 Ledger — density is the moat, photos stay.** Build the hybrid decision-row; don't replace photos, don't ship a pure institutional table as the only view. *Confidence ~65–88% depending on default-vs-toggle.*
- **T4 Builder — now a focus call, not a brokenness call.** Zoning is indexed and BuilderColumns exists, so "it's broken" no longer holds. The strategic case to **de-emphasize** it (a solo founder can't serve land-assemblers) still stands — but it's reversible, so just drop it from the default persona switcher rather than ripping it out. *Confidence ~70%.*
- **T5 Consumer funnels — consolidate to one, decide if it belongs.** The runtime error is fixed, so the urgency dropped, but maintaining two overlapping home-value funnels is still waste. A homeowner-value funnel is the best seller-lead magnet for a referral agent — but it contradicts "filter out casuals," so own that contradiction. *Confidence ~70%.*
- **T6 Identity — make it coherent AND premium.** One verb, one membership noun, one gate name. Auto-resolves once T1+T2 are decided. Don't flatten to generic "just enter email." *Confidence ~88%.*

## The 3 things that, if unfixed, cap growth (consensus)

1. **No working path from cold visitor → a lead you can act on.** The funnel captures intent and throws it away, hides the viewable terminal behind a form, and your best seller-magnet is buried. Fix: one-step gate that routes intent to you + feature the (now-working) home-value funnel. (Resolves T1, T2, T5, T6.)
2. **Trust-killing lies on the value-prop surfaces.** Fabricated cashflow on land (C2) and white/unreadable pages (C1) each tell a sharp investor "the math here is theater." The whole pitch is "trust our numbers." (C3 and C10 — two of the original four — are already fixed; finish C1+C2.)
3. **The unresolved audience contradiction.** "Realtor.ca traffic AND filter 99%" / "Bloomberg density AND yes/no-deal flipper" can't both be the target. Pick **flipper/investor lead-gen for a referral business** and let it kill half the backlog (builder zoning depth, dense-table-as-default, velvet-rope theater).

## Appendix A — Red-Team (prosecution) write-up, verbatim

> Role: ruthless growth/product strategist, instructed to attack both the spec and the panel. Findings reconciled above; some bug states have since changed (see table).

### Brutal one-paragraph thesis
The spec is two products wearing one trench coat, and neither one is the business. "Bloomberg Terminal for Canadian Real Estate" is an aesthetic the founder *wants to build*; "Brampton flipper who needs to know is-this-a-deal-yes/no, monetized by my own EXP licence" is the business he *actually has*. These collide hardest at the funnel: a solo licensed agent makes money when a high-intent local buyer/seller raises their hand and lets him represent them — that is a *volume-of-qualified-leads* game — yet the product is architected to *manufacture scarcity and repel 99% of visitors*. You cannot simultaneously want Realtor.ca-scale traffic and brag (falsely, in code) that "most applications are declined." The Velvet Rope isn't exclusivity, it's a lead-gen leak: two walls (a fake-review form **then** a magic link) that discard the very intent they capture, in front of a Terminal that's already anonymously viewable. The panel is mostly right that this caps the funnel, but the panel also over-indexes on institutional-grade polish (dense numeric tables, builder fixes, a11y) that a solo referral-driven founder cannot cash. The real cap on growth isn't the heatmap legend or div-onClick — it's that there is no working path from "cold visitor" to "this guy contacts me about a Brampton property," and two of the three "value" funnels are literally throwing errors or rendering white. Fix the money path; stop polishing the terminal cosplay.

### The settled bugs — triage for a solo founder
- **C1 (white pages, invisible text):** Fix-this-week. Broken-white pages read as "abandoned/broken" — poisons trust on the exact value-prop pages a referral lead lands on. Cheap to fix, high blast radius.
- **C2 (phantom "-$311/mo cashflow" on VACANT LAND):** Fix-this-week. The single most credibility-lethal bug for the one persona that matters. A calculator that fabricates a number on land proves the math can't be trusted anywhere. Gate by property type; show nothing rather than a lie.
- **C3 (fake "real-time" analytics):** Fix-eventually — but un-ship it NOW. Orphan route; hide/noindex this week, rebuild later. *(Now fixed — a real analytics surface shipped.)*
- **C4 (mobile truncation/overlap):** Fix-this-week. Flipper traffic is majority mobile; "$2…" on the flagship surface is a bounce. Minimum: a mobile card fallback.
- **C8 (rows lack keyboard/focus):** Ignore-for-now. Matters for institutional procurement/screen-readers — not the near-term buyer. Roll in free on the next row rebuild.
- **C10 (heatmap legend lies):** Fix-eventually, worse than it looks — a §10 "equivalent-not-better" failure dressed as a feature. If you can't bind color to a metric in <1 day, remove the legend's metric claim. *(Now fixed — color binds to the active metric.)*

Net: C1, C2, C4 this week; C3 hide-now/rebuild-later; C10 de-claim now; C8 ignore.

### Verdicts on tensions
- **T1 Velvet rope — Panel right, 88%.** Exclusivity is fictional (100% admitted; "most declined" false in code), then dumps the user at a second wall and discards intent. Collapse to one step; keep one intent question that qualifies the lead for *you*; magic-link in the same flow; route the intent to an email/CRM row. Kill the "most applications declined" copy — it's a liability. The founder's instinct to *capture intent* is right; the execution trashes it.
- **T2 Zero-TTV — Panel right, 90%.** Spec says "drop the user into the data," then forces a form first with no terminal link. Opening the terminal doesn't cannibalize exclusivity because the exclusive thing (sold/VOW rows) is already gated *inside* it. Add "Explore the Terminal"; gate sold/VOW behind the one-step auth at the moment of intent.
- **T3 Cards vs ledger — side AGAINST the panel's framing, 62%.** A flipper triages on photos AND a few hard numbers, not a bond-trader's numeric-only table. But a pure photo list is merely equivalent to HouseSigma (§10 forbids). Build the hybrid: keep the photo, make each card a decision row with 3–4 shadow-data numbers as badges, plus an optional "dense mode" toggle. Differentiation is *which numbers per row*, not *removing photos*.
- **T4 Builder — CUT, 93%.** Non-functional, memo says cut, furthest possible audience from a solo flipper referral base. Cut from nav/switcher; archive the spec section. Building PostGIS overlays for a persona you can't monetize is the textbook waste of a solo founder's scarcest resource. *(Reconciliation note: zoning is now indexed and BuilderColumns exists, so the "non-functional" basis is weaker — but the focus argument stands.)*
- **T5 Two funnels — pick /whats-my-home-hiding, 70%.** A homeowner value + reno-upside funnel is the best fit for a referral agent (seller leads are gold). But it contradicts "filter out casuals" — because a homeowner checking value *is* the casual user. Make the free public one THE funnel, fix the error, stop pretending you filter casuals. Delete /hidden-equity. Compliance: VOW-derived valuations shown publicly need BoR sign-off — lead with reno/value-add framing, gate the comp-derived AVM.
- **T6 Identity incoherence — Panel right, 85%.** Five labels for one action; /login copy demolishes the velvet-rope premise. Auto-resolves once T1+T2 are decided. Pick one verb ("Get access"/"Sign in"); drop "Apply"/"Members only."

### Where the founder's instinct beats the panel
- Capturing investor INTENT is genuinely right (vs. "kill the friction entirely") — a one-question intent capture is the highest-ROI element for a referral business. Don't accept a pure 1-click Google login; it captures nothing.
- NOT building a brokerage / monetizing via existing licence is correct; the panel audits this like a VC-funded SaaS. The product is a *lead magnet*, not the revenue engine — that reframing invalidates a chunk of the backlog.
- Refusing to be "just another photo portal" (§10) is right; density isn't the differentiator — *shadow-data-per-row* is.
- The shadow-data thesis (True DOM, suite potential, price compression) is the actual moat and the panel under-celebrates it. Protect it.

### Recommendations I would VETO
- The full dense Bloomberg numeric table as the *default* ledger — ship as opt-in toggle at most.
- C8 keyboard/focus a11y as a near-term priority — roll in opportunistically.
- Rebuilding /analytics into a real "real-time" dashboard *now* — hide the orphan, rebuild later. *(Moot — already rebuilt.)*
- Any further Builder/Developer investment (PostGIS, $/buildable-sqft) — cut, don't fix.
- Doubling down on "we decline most applicants" — false, repels referral leads, regulatory smell.

### The 3 growth caps
1. No working path from cold visitor → an actionable lead (resolves T1, T2, T5, T6).
2. Two trust-killing lies on the value-prop surfaces (C2, C10, plus white pages C1).
3. The unresolved audience contradiction — pick flipper/investor lead-gen and let it kill half the backlog.

## Appendix B — Spec-Defender (contrarian) write-up, verbatim

> Role: defend the deliberate spec choices as the moat. Findings reconciled above.

### One-paragraph thesis
The panel ran a competent consumer-SaaS audit on a product that is explicitly not a consumer-SaaS product, and most of its "fixes" are funnel-widening moves that optimize a metric this business does not sell (raw traffic / signup conversion) at the direct expense of the two metrics it does sell (qualified-lead quality and founder trust). For a solo, licence-monetized, referral-driven operator, the unit of value is not a visitor — it is a high-intent investor who closes a transaction. Friction, density, and a deliberately hostile-to-tourists posture are not UX debt; they are the qualification filter and the trust signal that make a sophisticated buyer believe this terminal knows something HouseSigma doesn't. That said, the defense is not absolute: several findings are genuine bugs that destroy the very credibility the strategy depends on, and one piece of copy is an outright lie that no exclusivity argument can rescue. Concede those fast, fight hard for the rest.

### Concede: the bugs and the indefensible bits
- **C1 (theme leak):** Indefensible. A terminal whose flagship pages render white-on-white fails the credibility test on contact. Move theme to `<body>`.
- **C2 (phantom cashflow on land):** Indefensible and worse than a generic bug — a wrong number in the financial engine that *is* the moat. Gate the underwriting model by property type.
- **C3 (fake "real-time"):** Indefensible — most dangerous item for this founder. Wire to the real region aggregates (which already exist) or take the route down. *(Now done.)*
- **C4 (mobile truncation):** Concede as a bug, with a priority asterisk. Prices must never truncate.
- **C8 (rows lack keyboard/focus):** Concede. Power users live on the keyboard — this *helps* the moat.
- **C10 (heatmap legend lies):** Indefensible — a visualization that lies. Bind color to a metric or remove the claim. *(Now done — color binds to the metric.)*
- **The "most applications declined" copy:** Fully concede, loudly. If everyone is admitted, the line is literally false — exclusivity *theater* is legitimate, *fraud* is not, and a licensed realtor making demonstrably false marketing statements carries RECO risk. Kill the line.

### Defend each tension
- **T1 — DEFEND, 80%.** The payoff of the 3 steps is not gatekeeping; it's segmentation/self-selection. A user who answers "primary investment strategy" has told you what they are, invested sunk cost (a qualified lead worth your scarce time), and primed themselves to read the product as an instrument. For a referral business, a smaller pool of strategy-tagged investors beats 10× un-segmented emails (the Superhuman/early-Robinhood pattern). Refinement: the *second login* is a real defect — make the final application step *be* the magic-link account. Collapse the redundant auth, not the three questions.
- **T2 — mostly CONCEDE, 55% defense.** The spec's own "Zero-TTV" contradicts forcing a form before any number. The "Explore the Terminal" link is *consistent* with the spec. Nuance: show the analytical surface, gate the *shadow data* (the locked-teaser model already in the codebase, which also satisfies VOW). On balance the panel wins here.
- **T3 — DEFEND HARD, 88%.** The dense numeric table is what the spec already demands; the photo-card list is the part that drifted *toward* HouseSigma. The ledger is the moat. Build a dense, sortable, keyboard-navigable numeric table as the *primary* list view; photo-cards as a toggle. This is the rare case where the panel's literal recommendation strengthens the moat.
- **T4 — CONCEDE, 75% it should go now.** A solo founder can't credibly serve land-assembly developers as a side feature; a half-built Builder lens dilutes the §10 bar. Cut from navigation now; keep the ambition as a Phase-N flag, not a live persona. Don't delete the *concept* from strategy — just stop presenting it as working.
- **T5 — CONCEDE with a naming caveat, 70%.** Two consumer funnels for a product whose thesis is "we filter out casuals" is incoherent on its own terms. Consolidate — but first decide whether *either* belongs on the analytical surface, or should live as a separate lightweight lead-capture page. Don't let "merge them" smuggle the consumer funnel deeper into the terminal.
- **T6 — CONCEDE, 90%.** Incoherent labels read as *unfinished*, not *exclusive*. Exclusivity requires a consistent ritual: one gate name, one membership noun, one verb ("Apply for Terminal Access" → "Member" → "Enter"). Make it coherent *and* premium — not coherent *and* generic.

### Panel recommendations that would HARM the moat
- Collapsing the velvet rope to a single email field — throws away the strategy tagging that makes each lead worth your time; converts a qualified pipeline into a newsletter list.
- Replacing the ledger with photo-cards "to match HouseSigma" — amputates the clearest differentiator; equivalence is death (§10).
- Full "show everything, gate nothing" — breaks VOW and gives the moat's payload to tourists/competitors for free. Show the terminal; never ungate the shadow data.
- Softening the anti-consumer posture into a "welcoming" consumer funnel — regresses toward the generic-portal mean the strategy exists to resist.
- De-prioritizing keyboard/density "because most users won't use them" — for the top-1% analytical persona, keyboard-driven density is table stakes.

### The strongest version of the strategy
1. Fix every C-bug first (credibility = the moat's foundation).
2. Kill the false-scarcity copy today; keep the ritual, lose the lie.
3. One velvet rope, harvested once (third step = magic-link; tag every lead by strategy).
4. Show the terminal, gate the shadow data (reconciles Zero-TTV with the velvet rope and VOW).
5. Make the dense ledger the hero; photo-cards as a toggle.
6. Cut what dilutes (Builder lens; consolidate consumer funnels off the analytical surface).
7. One coherent premium identity (Apply → Member → Enter).

Net: every concession buys *credibility*; every defended position protects *qualification, density, and the deliberate filtering of tourists*. Applied wholesale the audit produces a trustworthy generic portal; applied surgically it produces a trustworthy *terminal*. The difference is the entire business.
